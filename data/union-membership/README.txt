Not legal advice. These are survey estimates from the Current Population Survey, rounded to thousands and one decimal by BLS; small states have wider sampling error. The 2025 annual figures average the 11 months BLS published (January to September, November and December). BLS says the 2025 figures are not strictly comparable with other years.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/union-membership/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state code (50 states and DC), or US
  level: us or state
  stateName: State name (United States on the national record)
  year: The latest year in the BLS release
  status: P: the BLS figure as printed
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: Percent of employed wage and salary workers who are union members (the same as members_pct)
  unit: Unit of value
  employed_k: Employed wage and salary workers, thousands (sole or principal job; self-employed excluded)
  members_k: Members of a labor union or an employee association similar to a union, thousands
  members_pct: members_k as a percent of employed_k (BLS)
  represented_k: Union members plus workers with no union affiliation whose jobs are covered by a union or employee association contract, thousands
  represented_pct: represented_k as a percent of employed_k (BLS)
  previousYear: The year before (the second year of the release)
  employed_k_prev: employed_k in previousYear
  members_k_prev: members_k in previousYear
  members_pct_prev: members_pct in previousYear
  represented_k_prev: represented_k in previousYear
  represented_pct_prev: represented_pct in previousYear
  change_members_pct_pts: members_pct minus members_pct_prev, percentage points
  effectiveFrom: When the fact took effect (YYYY-MM-DD), if known
  effectiveTo: When it stopped applying; empty while current
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
