Not legal advice. These are price and employment statistics from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, kept for marketing planning. Index values are not a fee schedule, and BLS revises recent months.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/legal-prices/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  series: BLS series id (for example CUUR0000SEGC01)
  measure: What the series measures
  program: cpi (consumer prices), ppi (producer prices) or ces (employment)
  month: The reference month, YYYY-MM
  status: P primary source, S secondary source, D default (no specific rule found), U open question or withheld
  value: The published index value, or jobs in thousands, dollars or hours (see unit)
  unit: Unit of value
  baseNote: The index base period as BLS publishes it
  seasonal: SA seasonally adjusted, NSA not adjusted
  preliminary: true while BLS may still revise the value
  yoyPct: Change from the same month a year earlier, percent, one decimal
  momPct: Change from the previous month, percent, one decimal
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
