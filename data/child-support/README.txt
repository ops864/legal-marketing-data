Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state or territory code, or US
  level: us or state (every state, DC, Guam, Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands)
  area: US, or the 2-digit state FIPS code
  areaName: Jurisdiction name
  stateName: Jurisdiction name
  status: P: OCSE's own preliminary tables
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: Dollars of current support distributed per 100 dollars due in the latest fiscal year (the same as current_paid_per_100_due_latest)
  unit: Unit of value
  fiscalYear: Latest federal fiscal year in the file (October to September)
  firstYear: First fiscal year in the file for this jurisdiction
  collections_usd_latest: Child support collections distributed, in US dollars (Table P-4)
  cases_open_latest: Cases open at the end of the fiscal year (Table P-53)
  cases_orders_latest: Cases with a support order established (Table P-64)
  paternities_latest: Paternities established or acknowledged (Table P-71)
  cases_collection_latest: Cases in which a collection was made on an obligation (Table P-75)
  current_due_usd_latest: Current support due in the fiscal year, in US dollars (Table P-83)
  current_paid_usd_latest: Support distributed as current support, in US dollars (Table P-84)
  arrears_due_usd_latest: Arrears due for all fiscal years, in US dollars (Table P-85)
  cases_arrears_latest: Cases with arrears due (Table P-87)
  children_latest: Children in child support cases (Table P-93)
  cost_effectiveness_latest: OCSE's cost-effectiveness ratio: dollars collected per dollar spent (Table P-36)
  orders_per_100_open_latest: Computed here: cases with a support order per 100 cases open
  current_paid_per_100_due_latest: Computed here: dollars of current support distributed per 100 dollars due
  effectiveFrom: First day of the first fiscal year
  effectiveTo: Last day of the latest fiscal year
  lastChecked: Date the file was last re-read from OCSE
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
