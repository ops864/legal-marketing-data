Not legal advice. These are bid bands and competition levels from Google's keyword data on the date shown, kept for research and marketing planning. They are not a price quote, and the cost of any campaign depends on its own settings.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/ad-costs/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: US for the whole country, else the two-letter code of a state or DC; together the records cover the whole US, all 50 states and DC
  stateName: The place's name: United States, or the name of the state or DC
  level: family (one query family in one place) or tier (US: families grouped by searches a month)
  status: P primary source, S secondary source
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: The machine value (see unit)
  unit: Unit of value
  asOf: Date of the Keyword Planner export the figures come from
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
