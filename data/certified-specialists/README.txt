Not legal advice. Not a recommendation of any lawyer. These are counts of certifications on official state lists, as the lists stood on the dates shown; a lawyer can hold more than one certification, and certification is voluntary, so the counts say nothing about the quality of lawyers who are not certified.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/certified-specialists/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state code
  level: practice (one practice area and certifying body) or state (all areas together)
  stateName: 
  year: Year of the count: Minnesota's year end, Tennessee's read date
  practice: Our practice-area id (all-areas on state rows)
  practiceLabel: Practice area in plain words
  specialty: The area as the source words it
  certifier: The certifying body, as the source names it
  status: P: the official list's own figure
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: Certified specialists (certifications held)
  unit: What value counts
  effectiveFrom: When the fact took effect (YYYY-MM-DD), if known
  effectiveTo: When it stopped applying; empty while current
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
