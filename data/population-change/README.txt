Not legal advice. These are Census Bureau estimates built from birth and death records, tax and Medicare files and survey data, not counts of court cases or estates. A death is not a probate filing: many estates pass without one. Rates and shares are for planning, not for any one person's matter.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/population-change/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state code (50 states and DC), or US
  level: us, state or county
  area: US, the 2-digit state FIPS code or the 5-digit county FIPS code (Connecticut: planning regions)
  areaName: County and state, state name, or United States
  stateName: State name (United States on the national record)
  year: The estimates vintage: figures run to July 1 of this year
  period: The latest estimate year: July 1 of the year before to June 30 of the vintage year
  status: P: the Census Bureau's own estimate
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: Deaths in the latest estimate year (the same as deaths)
  unit: Unit of value
  population: Residents on July 1 of the vintage year
  population_base: Residents on the April 1, 2020 estimates base
  population_change_pct: Change in residents since April 1, 2020, percent
  deaths: Deaths in the latest estimate year
  deaths_5y: Deaths in the five estimate years from July 1, 2020
  death_rate: Deaths per 1,000 residents in the latest year (Census Bureau rate)
  births: Births in the latest estimate year
  births_5y: Births in the five estimate years from July 1, 2020
  birth_rate: Births per 1,000 residents in the latest year (Census Bureau rate)
  natural_change: Births minus deaths in the latest year
  international_migration: Net international migration in the latest year
  international_migration_5y: Net international migration over the five estimate years
  international_migration_rate: Net international migration per 1,000 residents (Census Bureau rate)
  net_migration: International plus domestic migration in the latest year
  net_migration_5y: Net migration over the five estimate years
  net_migration_rate: Net migration per 1,000 residents (Census Bureau rate)
  age_65plus: Residents aged 65 and over on July 1 of the vintage year
  age_65plus_pct: age_65plus as a percentage of residents
  age_65plus_base: Residents aged 65 and over on April 1, 2020
  age_65plus_change_pct: Change in residents 65 and over since April 1, 2020, percent
  age_75plus: Residents aged 75 and over
  age_75plus_pct: age_75plus as a percentage of residents
  age_85plus: Residents aged 85 and over
  age_85plus_pct: age_85plus as a percentage of residents
  effectiveFrom: When the fact took effect (YYYY-MM-DD), if known
  effectiveTo: When it stopped applying; empty while current
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
