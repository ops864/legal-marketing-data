Not legal advice. These are counts of what the public home and practice pages of law-firm websites showed when they were read; a feature on a page we did not read is not counted, and a website is placed in the state of its first office address found.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/law-firm-websites/

Columns:
  id: Stable row id
  group: The group key (ALL, STATE-<code> or PRACTICE-<practice>), then the measure
  groupType: The measure
  segmentType: all, state or practice
  segment: The state code or practice the row is about
  value: The measure: a share in percent rounded to one decimal, a count, or websites per 100,000 residents
  unit: What value measures
  websites: US law-firm websites in the group (N); every state and practice group has at least 100
  text: The row in one sentence (the per-100,000 rows also name the state's third)
  version: Dataset version
  lastChecked: Date the websites were last read
  sourceLabels: Source label
  sourceUrls: Source URL
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
