Not legal advice. These are counts state agencies publish, kept for research and marketing planning. A complaint or charge is not a finding that discrimination happened; rights and deadlines are for your own counsel.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/state-civil-rights/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state code
  level: 
  stateName: 
  agency: The state agency that published the figure
  measure: What is counted: intake-forms, immediate-right-to-sue, charges-received, employment-charges, charges-docketed or employment-allegations
  period: Calendar or state fiscal year the figure covers
  status: P primary source, S secondary source, D default (no specific rule found), U open question or withheld
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: The figure as the agency prints it
  unit: What the figure counts (units differ by state)
  quote: The verbatim words in the document that carry the figure (for Pennsylvania, the portal query and its result)
  document: The report or table the figure comes from
  documentUrl: Where the document is
  verified: The date the quote was last found in the document
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  note: Caveats
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
