Not legal advice. This is a plain-English summary of public ethics opinions, kept for marketing planning, and opinions and rules change. Check with your state bar and your own counsel before you run an ad.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/opinions/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state code, DC, US (federal), ABA, or PLATFORM:<name>
  jurisdictionName: Jurisdiction name
  body: The body that issued the opinion
  opinionType: formal, advisory, informal, committee-guideline or court
  bindingWeight: How much weight it carries: advisory-committee, committee-guideline or court
  title: The opinion’s title
  topics: Topic keys, separated by "; "
  opinionStatus: in-force, modified or withdrawn
  status: P primary source, S secondary source
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  whatToDo: What a law firm should do about it, in one sentence
  url: The official link
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
  number: The opinion number, if it has one (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  issued: The date issued (YYYY-MM-DD, or the year or month when only that is known) (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  rules: The professional conduct rules it applies, separated by "; " (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  quote: A short exact quote from the source (25 words or fewer) (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  detail: A short sourced detail on the record (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  ai_chatbot_disclosure: AI opinions only: must a chatbot or AI agent say it is not a lawyer or is an AI? yes, brief, no, excluded, or blank (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  ai_generated_ads: AI opinions only: do the advertising rules reach AI-made ad content (copy, images, voice)? yes, brief, no, excluded, or blank (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  ai_intake_solicitation: AI opinions only: does AI intake or outreach raise solicitation limits (Rule 7.3)? yes, brief, no, excluded, or blank (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  ai_superiority_claims: AI opinions only: can a firm claim its AI or results are better (Rule 7.1)? yes, brief, no, excluded, or blank (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  confidentiality_in_marketing_tools: AI opinions only: does it address prospects’ confidentiality in chatbots and marketing tools? yes, brief, no, excluded, or blank (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  fees_for_ai: AI opinions only: does it say how AI affects fees and charges? yes, brief, no, excluded, or blank (in opinions-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
