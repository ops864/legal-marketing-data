Not legal advice. These are counts of reports that mine operators file with MSHA, not findings of fault or of a violation. A report is a row of MSHA's file: an injured worker, an illness or an accident without injury. The latest year is partial. Workers' compensation claims and deadlines are for your own counsel.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/mine-accidents/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state or territory code, or US
  level: us, state, county or county-not-recorded
  status: P: MSHA's own record
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: Reports (the same as reports)
  unit: Unit of value
  reports: Accident, injury and illness reports filed with MSHA
  fatalities: Reports with degree of injury fatality
  permanent_disability: Permanent total or partial disability
  lost_time: Days away from work, with or without restricted activity
  restricted_only: Days of restricted activity only
  no_lost_time: No days away and no restricted activity
  other: All other reports: accident only, occupational illness, natural causes, nonemployees, first aid
  dataThrough: Date of the last accident in the file
  effectiveFrom: When the fact took effect (YYYY-MM-DD), if known
  effectiveTo: When it stopped applying; empty while current
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
  stateName:  (in mine-accidents-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  county: Five-digit county FIPS code of the mine (in mine-accidents-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  countyName: County name (in mine-accidents-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  year: Calendar year of the accident (state and US records) (in mine-accidents-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  reports_coal: Reports at coal mines (in mine-accidents-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  reports_metal: Reports at metal and nonmetal mines (in mine-accidents-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  fatalities_coal: Deaths at coal mines (in mine-accidents-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  fatalities_metal: Deaths at metal and nonmetal mines (in mine-accidents-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
