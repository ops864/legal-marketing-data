Not legal advice. These are counts from public datasets divided by the number of private law-office establishments; a law office is an establishment, not a lawyer, and a state with a high ratio is not necessarily short of lawyers.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Columns:
  id: Stable row id
  group: <area>-<measure>: US or STATE-<code>, then RESIDENTS, BANKRUPTCY, FATAL-CRASHES, DIVORCES, SPANISH-LEP, EVICTIONS or OVERALL
  groupType: The measure (column key)
  segment: US or the state code
  areaType: us or state
  value: The measure: a ratio rounded to one decimal (residents per office as a whole number), or the state's overall rank
  unit: What value measures, with its period
  tier: Third of states on this measure (highest third = most demand per law office); national total on the US rows; for evictions, a third of the states whose eviction data is recent
  law_offices: Private law-office establishments, 2026 Q1 (the denominator of every ratio)
  text: The row in one sentence
  version: Dataset version
  lastChecked: Date computed
  sourceLabels: Source label
  sourceUrls: Source URL
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
