Not legal advice. These are counts of accidents recorded by the NTSB, not findings of fault: a probable cause, if any, is in the NTSB's own report. An accident is an event meeting the NTSB's definition, which includes events with no injury. Counts for the latest year are preliminary and are revised as the NTSB closes cases. Claims and deadlines are for your own counsel.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state or territory code, or US
  level: state or us
  stateName: 
  year: Calendar year of the accident
  status: P: the NTSB's own record
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: Accidents (the same as accidents)
  unit: Unit of value
  accidents: Civil aviation accidents recorded by the NTSB (not incidents)
  fatal_accidents: Accidents with at least one death
  fatalities: Deaths in those accidents
  serious_injuries: Serious injuries
  minor_injuries: Minor injuries
  accidents_ga: Accidents of general aviation (FAR Part 91)
  accidents_airline: Accidents of scheduled airlines (Part 121)
  accidents_ondemand: Accidents of commuter and on-demand operations (Part 135)
  accidents_agri: Accidents of agricultural operations (Part 137)
  accidents_other: Accidents of all other operations (non-U.S.-registered, public, military, unmanned, unclassified)
  fatalities_ga: Deaths in general aviation accidents
  fatalities_airline: Deaths in airline accidents
  fatalities_ondemand: Deaths in commuter and on-demand accidents
  fatalities_agri: Deaths in agricultural accidents
  fatalities_other: Deaths in other accidents
  accidents_outside_states: US records: accidents offshore or with no state, counted in the US total only
  fatalities_outside_states: US records: deaths in those accidents
  dataThrough: Last date in the NTSB file
  effectiveFrom: When the fact took effect (YYYY-MM-DD), if known
  effectiveTo: When it stopped applying; empty while current
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
