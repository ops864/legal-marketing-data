Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: US or the two-letter state code (DC included); a county's state
  level: us, state or county
  area: US, the state code or the 5-digit county FIPS code
  areaName: Area name
  year: The last year of the ACS 5-year period (2024 = 2020-2024)
  status: P published by the Census Bureau
  total: Total residents (B05001_001)
  citizenBornUS: U.S. citizens born in the United States (B05001_002)
  citizenBornTerritory: U.S. citizens born in Puerto Rico or U.S. Island Areas (B05001_003)
  citizenBornAbroad: U.S. citizens born abroad of American parent(s) (B05001_004)
  naturalized: U.S. citizens by naturalization (B05001_005)
  naturalizedMoe: Margin of error of the naturalized count, 90% confidence
  noncitizen: Residents who are not U.S. citizens (B05001_006)
  noncitizenMoe: Margin of error of the noncitizen count, 90% confidence
  noncitizenPct: Noncitizens as a percent of total residents, one decimal
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  unit: Unit of value
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
  county: 5-digit county FIPS code (county rows) (in noncitizen-population-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
  countyName:  (in noncitizen-population-values.csv, as id, column and value rows)
