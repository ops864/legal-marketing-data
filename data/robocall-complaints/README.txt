Not legal advice. These are counts of complaints people filed with the FCC, kept for marketing planning. A complaint is a report, not a finding that anyone broke a law, and no phone number or complaint text is published. Texting and calling rules (the TCPA and state laws) are for your own counsel.
Probative is a marketing firm, not a law firm; not legal advice.

Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/robocall-complaints/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state code, DC, US (federal), ABA, or PLATFORM:<name>
  jurisdictionName: Jurisdiction name
  measure: text (text-message complaints) or call (phone-call complaints, including those with no method given)
  status: P primary source, S secondary source, D default (no specific rule found), U open question or withheld
  value: Complaints in the last 12 complete months
  unit: Unit of value
  per100k: Complaints per 100,000 residents (Census Vintage 2025 estimate for July 1, 2025); empty for territories the Census file does not cover
  population: 
  previous12: Complaints in the 12 months before that
  yoyPct: Change from the previous 12 months, in percent
  referenceYear2024: Complaints in calendar year 2024, a full year with no gap in the file
  referenceYear2024Per100k: The same per 100,000 residents (2024 estimate)
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  effectiveFrom: When the fact took effect (YYYY-MM-DD), if known
  lastChecked: The day it was last confirmed (YYYY-MM-DD)
  note: Caveats
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
