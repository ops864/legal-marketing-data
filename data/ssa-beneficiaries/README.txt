Human page: https://probativegrowth.com/data/ssa-beneficiaries/

Columns:
  id: Stable record id (never reused)
  jurisdiction: Two-letter state or territory code, or US
  level: us or state (every state, DC and five territories)
  area: US, or the 2-digit state FIPS code
  areaName: Jurisdiction name
  stateName: Jurisdiction name
  status: P: SSA's own published tables
  text: The record in one plain-English sentence
  value: Beneficiaries in current-payment status in the month (the same as total_count)
  unit: Unit of value
  month: Month of the tables (December 2025)
  total_count: Beneficiaries: total (Table 2)
  retired_workers_count: Beneficiaries: retired workers (Table 2)
  retired_spouses_count: Beneficiaries: retired spouses (Table 2)
  retired_children_count: Beneficiaries: retired children (Table 2)
  survivor_widows_parents_count: Beneficiaries: survivor widows parents (Table 2)
  survivor_children_count: Beneficiaries: survivor children (Table 2)
  disabled_workers_count: Beneficiaries: disabled workers (Table 2)
  disabled_spouses_count: Beneficiaries: disabled spouses (Table 2)
  disabled_children_count: Beneficiaries: disabled children (Table 2)
  men_65_plus_count: Beneficiaries: men 65 plus (Table 2)
  women_65_plus_count: Beneficiaries: women 65 plus (Table 2)
  total_amount_k: Monthly benefits for total, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  retired_workers_amount_k: Monthly benefits for retired workers, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  retired_spouses_amount_k: Monthly benefits for retired spouses, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  retired_children_amount_k: Monthly benefits for retired children, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  survivor_widows_parents_amount_k: Monthly benefits for survivor widows parents, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  survivor_children_amount_k: Monthly benefits for survivor children, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  disabled_workers_amount_k: Monthly benefits for disabled workers, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  disabled_spouses_amount_k: Monthly benefits for disabled spouses, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  disabled_children_amount_k: Monthly benefits for disabled children, in thousands of dollars (Table 3)
  avg_monthly_benefit: Average monthly benefit in dollars: total amount x 1,000 / total beneficiaries (computed here)
  avg_retired_worker_benefit: Average monthly retired-worker benefit in dollars (computed here)
  avg_disabled_worker_benefit: Average monthly disabled-worker benefit in dollars (computed here)
  effectiveFrom: First day of the month
  effectiveTo: Last day of the month
  lastChecked: Date the tables were last read from SSA
  sourceLabels: Sources, separated by " | "
  sourceUrls: Source URLs, in the same order
  confidence: high, medium or low: computed from status, a second check, age against the refresh cadence and sampling error (rule: /data/#confidence)
  confidenceBasis: The reasons for the confidence level, in a short phrase
